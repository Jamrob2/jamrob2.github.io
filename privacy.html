This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Weekly Budget Meal Planner ("the app"), made by [YOUR NAME] ("I", "me").

What the app stores

Your meal plans, recipes, food preferences, budget, prices you enter, freezer list and settings are stored only on your device. I do not run a server for the app, do not create accounts and cannot see your data.

What the app collects

The app does not collect, sell or share personal information. It has no ads, analytics or tracking.

The app loads its typeface from Google Fonts. When it does, your device connects to Google's servers, which may log your IP address as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Backups

If you use Back up, the app copies your data to your clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Start over, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. This permanently deletes everything stored by the app.

Prices and nutrition

Grocery prices are estimates and may not match your store. Nutrition figures are approximate. Check labels and prices before you buy, and talk to a health professional about dietary needs or allergies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
